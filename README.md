## About

High school student (*Liceo Scientifico — Scienze Applicate*, Italy) focused on **cybersecurity, Linux systems engineering, local AI workloads, and embedded hardware**. I build, test, and harden environments from first principles—focusing on performance, reliability, and security on constrained hardware.

* **Offensive & Defensive Security** — Isolated red-team lab exercises, Linux system hardening, threat detection, and secure service deployment.
* **Systems & Infrastructure** — Bare-metal Linux administration, Docker containerization, automated deployments, and self-hosted tooling.
* **Applied AI & Local Inference** — Quantized LLM, VLM, and TTS pipelines on consumer GPUs, audio DSP tooling, and experimental PyTorch architectures.
* **Hardware & Prototyping** — Microcontroller firmware (ESP32, ESP8266, Arduino), biometric/environmental sensor integration, and parametric CAD in OpenSCAD.
